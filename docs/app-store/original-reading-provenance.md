# Original bilingual reading passages

Date: 2026-09-06

The six English passages in `app/Resources/original-reading.json` were independently composed for this app with AI assistance. Their Chinese translations were created alongside them for this app. No source article, existing literary work, or third-party translation was used to write or translate these passages. The passages are fictional everyday learning scenarios; their people and events are invented.

The app labels their source as `AI 辅助创作`. Each `source_url` is empty because these passages have no external source article. This asset does not grant or assert any third-party license, and this provenance record does not represent human legal review or guarantee legal clearance.
